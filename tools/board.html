The distance-transform fixture improved about 8%. Full pilot timing changed about 0.5%, which does not establish a useful speed gain. Output hashes stayed identical.

NORMALS SCORE
Attribute-aware reduction is the priority.

Strict normal preservation allows little reduction: three of eight chains stayed unchanged. Strong coverage results do not establish strong shading fidelity.

The visual contract
32 → 16 px · 8 levels

Examples use a 2 → 3 px transition curve. Source-error budgets are checked separately. The same bounds and scale are used throughout each chain.

The audit behind these examples
12 orthographic + 4 perspective views

Coverage is checked at 4× sampling with refinement to 8×. Search uses a separate camera set. These are finite sampled views, not a universal error guarantee.

What remains outside this board
Full-quality corpus audit

The default 642 + 64 camera preset has not been run across the full corpus. Texture images, opacity, skinning and morph targets are outside this version.
